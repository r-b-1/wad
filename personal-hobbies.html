Hobbies & Interests

The same pattern shows up everywhere: I like understanding how systems behave.

Outside of class and project work, I still end up gravitating toward process, structure, and things people genuinely ship.

Open Source 01

Projects built for real use are always the most interesting.

I like browsing GitHub and finding products that escaped the prototype stage. Seeing what people actually decided to keep, cut, or simplify says a lot about good engineering.

Rabbit Hole 02

OpenClaw caught my attention recently.

An open source AI assistant that hooks into macOS Reminders, Messages, Telegram, and Slack. The interesting part isn’t just the idea; it’s the architecture of connecting a model to native operating system workflows.

Sports 03

I follow the NBA and Vikings with the same bias toward decision-making.

I care about why a team chooses a direction, how roles evolve over a season, and what small structural choices change outcomes over time.

What I Notice

Taste usually comes from paying attention longer than most people do.

That applies to codebases, product decisions, basketball rotations, and even which open source tools feel like they were made by people with actual standards.

I’m drawn to things that reveal more structure the closer you look.
